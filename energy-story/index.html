Storage that outlasts the weather.

Halden's iron-based battery holds clean power for days, not hours, so utilities can keep the lights on through storms, heat waves and long, windless weeks.

talk to team view projects

Our battery is built from iron, one of the most common materials on the planet. It stores energy by letting iron rust and releases it by reversing the rust, a simple reaction that turns cheap metal into days of dependable power for the grid.
